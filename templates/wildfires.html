WILDFIRES PREPARATION



Wildfires

Wildfires can ruin homes and cause injuries or death to people and animals. A wildfire is an unplanned fire that burns in a natural area such as a forest, grassland, or prairie. Wildfires can:
  • Often be caused by humans or lightning.
  • Cause flooding or disrupt transportation, gas, power, and communications.
  • Happen anywhere, anytime.
  • Cost governments billions each year.

IF YOU ARE UNDER A WILDFIRE WARNING, GET TO SAFETY RIGHT AWAY

  • Leave immediately if told to do so.
  • Listen for emergency alerts.
  • Use N95 masks to protect breathing.

Prepare NOW

  • Sign up for emergency alerts.
  • Know evacuation routes.
  • Prepare emergency supplies.
  • Seal rooms from outside air.
  • Store important documents safely.
  • Use fire-resistant materials.
  • Maintain water sources.
  • Create defensible space around your home.
  • Review insurance coverage.
  • Monitor air quality alerts.

Survive DURING

  • Evacuate immediately if ordered.
  • If trapped, call emergency services.
  • Follow official instructions.
  • Wear respiratory protection.
  • Stay indoors if smoke is present.

Be Safe AFTER

  • Return only when authorities say it is safe.
  • Avoid hot ash and smoldering debris.
  • Use messages instead of calls.
  • Wear protective gear during cleanup.
  • Document property damage.
  • Watch for flooding risks after fires.
