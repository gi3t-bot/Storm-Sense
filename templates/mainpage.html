TSUNAMI

A tsunami is an ocean wave triggered by large earthquakes that occur near or under the ocean, volcanic eruptions, submarine landslides, or by onshore landslides in which large volumes of debris fall into the water.

EARTHQUAKE

What is an earthquake? An earthquake is the sudden release of strain energy in the Earth's crust, resulting in waves of shaking that radiate outwards from the earthquake source. When two blocks of the earth suddenly slip past one another.

Learn More

FLOOD

A flood is an overflow of water that submerges normally dry land, occurring when water levels in rivers, lakes, or oceans rise beyond their usual limits, often due to heavy rain, snowmelt, or dam failures, causing significant damage and displacement.

Learn More

WILDFIRES

Wildfires (also known as bushfires, brush fires or forest fires) are large, uncontrolled and potentially destructive fires that can affect both rural and urban areas. They can spread quickly, change direction and even jump across large distances.

Learn More

FINANCIAL PREPAREDNESS

Financial preparedness is the proactive act of creating a financial safety net and plan to manage unexpected life events, like job loss or disasters, ensuring you can cover basic needs, maintain stability, and recover without falling into severe debt.

Learn More

YOUTH PREPAREDNESS

Youth preparedness involves teaching children and teens essential skills and knowledge to prepare for, respond to, and recover from disasters, empowering them to be resilient and actively support their families and communities.
