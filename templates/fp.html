FINANCIAL PREPAREDNESS



Financial Preparedness

  • Collect and secure critical financial, insurance, medical, and personal records to speed recovery after disasters.
  • Gather financial and critical personal, household, and medical information.
  • Maintain an emergency savings fund and keep small bills at home.
  • Review property, health, life, and flood insurance coverage.
  • Download and use the Emergency Financial First Aid Kit (EFFAK).

For Organizations

  • Host preparedness meetings.
  • Share EFFAK resources during staff sessions.
  • Include preparedness tips in newsletters.

At Home

  • Store documents securely in cloud, external drives, or safety deposit boxes.
  • Safeguard medical and financial records.

Household Identification

  • Photo IDs
  • Birth certificates
  • Social security cards
  • Military service records
  • Pet identification

Financial and Legal Documentation

  • Housing and payment records
  • Insurance policies
  • Income sources
  • Tax statements

Medical Information

  • Physician contacts
  • Health insurance details
  • Immunization records
  • Medication lists

Insurance Information

  • Document and insure home and business property.

Household Contact Information

  • Banks
  • Insurance agents
  • Healthcare providers
  • Service providers
  • Places of worship

Get Your Benefits Electronically

  • Switch to electronic payments for federal benefits.
  • Direct deposit to bank accounts.
  • Direct Express® prepaid debit card.
