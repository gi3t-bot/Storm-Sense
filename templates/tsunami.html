TSUNAMI PREPARATION



A tsunami can kill or injure people and damage or destroy buildings and infrastructure as waves come in and go out. A tsunami is a series of enormous ocean waves caused by earthquakes, underwater landslides, volcanic eruptions, or asteroids. Tsunamis can:
  • Travel 20–30 miles per hour with waves 10–100 feet high.
  • Cause flooding and disrupt transportation, power, communications, and water supply.
  • Happen anywhere along U.S. coasts, especially the Pacific and Caribbean.

IF YOU ARE UNDER A TSUNAMI WARNING:

  • First protect yourself from an earthquake. Drop, Cover, and Hold On.
  • Move to high ground as far inland as possible.
  • Watch for natural tsunami signs.
  • Listen to emergency alerts.
  • Evacuate immediately. Do not wait.
  • If in a boat, go out to sea.

Prepare NOW

  • Learn tsunami risks in coastal areas.
  • Know warning signs.
  • Practice evacuation routes.
  • Create a family communication plan.
  • Sign up for warning systems.
  • Consider flood and earthquake insurance.

Survive DURING

  • Protect yourself during earthquakes.
  • Move immediately to higher ground after shaking stops.
  • Follow evacuation routes.
  • Float on debris if in water.
  • Head out to sea if already on a boat.

Be Safe AFTER

  • Listen to local authorities.
  • Avoid floodwaters and damaged structures.
  • Watch for electrical hazards.
  • Document property damage.
  • Use texts instead of calls.
